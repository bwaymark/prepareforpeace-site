# About us

Prepare for Peace is a passion project, started in Nottingham in 2026. It is run by volunteers, with no funding and nothing to sell.

The idea grew out of time spent with Quakers, and much of it is coloured by Quaker thinking about peace, simplicity and looking after one another. But Prepare for Peace is not connected to Britain Yearly Meeting, any Area Meeting or any local Quaker meeting, and it does not speak for them. It belongs to everyone who takes part: a group of people trying to be helpful.

Our aim is simple. We want to build a free, practical guide to everything a community needs to look after itself and each other through hard times. That covers growing and storing food, keeping in touch when the phones go down, first aid, looking after pets, and the slower work of getting to know the people who live around you.

## For everyone

This site is for everyone. That means people of every faith and none, every political view, every background, every age and every level of experience. It also includes people who roll their eyes at the word "inclusivity". You are very welcome here too.

In a flood or a long power cut, nobody checks how their neighbours voted before helping them. We want this site to work the same way. We will keep the guides practical and free of jargon, and we will not use them to push anyone's politics.

## How the guides are made

Every guide is written or checked by someone who knows the subject well, from experience or training. Where good guides already exist, we would rather link to them than write our own. When we get something wrong, we want to hear about it so we can put it right.

We do our best, but we cannot promise that everything here is accurate, up to date, legal where you live, or even good advice. Please read our [disclaimer](/disclaimer/) before relying on any guide.

The words on this site are free to copy, share and improve. See our [licence](/licence/) for details.
