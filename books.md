# Books

Books we recommend for preparing well. We only list books that someone involved with this site has read and found useful. If you would like to suggest one, please [get in touch](/get-involved/).

## Health and medicine

### Where There Is No Doctor: A Village Health Care Handbook

By David Werner, with Carol Thuman and Jane Maxwell. Published by Hesperian Health Guides.

A classic, plainly written guide to looking after your own and your community's health when there is no doctor nearby. It covers how to examine someone who is ill, first aid, common illnesses, medicines and preventing disease. It has been used by community health workers around the world for decades.

- [Read it free online](https://en.hesperian.org/hhg/Where_There_Is_No_Doctor_Series) on Hesperian's HealthWiki
- [Buy the book or PDF](https://store.hesperian.org/products/where-there-is-no-doctor) from Hesperian

### Where There Is No Dentist

By Murray Dickson. Published by Hesperian Health Guides.

A companion to Where There Is No Doctor. It explains how to care for teeth and gums, how to prevent problems, and how to deal with common dental emergencies when you cannot reach a dentist.

- [Read it free online](https://en.hesperian.org/hhg/Where_There_Is_No_Dentist) on Hesperian's HealthWiki
- [Buy the book or PDF](https://store.hesperian.org/products/where-there-is-no-dentist) from Hesperian

<p class="notice">Both books were written for places with few health services. Some advice, especially on medicines, differs from UK practice. In the UK, always use 999 or NHS 111 when you can reach them.</p>
