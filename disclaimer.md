## Please use your own judgement

- Our guides are not a substitute for professional advice. For anything to do with health, the law, gas, electricity, structural work, firearms or animals, check with someone qualified.
- Laws and official guidance change, and they differ between England, Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland. Check the current rules before you act.
- If a guide does not seem right for you, your home or your community, do not follow it.
- If you think something on this site is wrong or unsafe, please [tell us](/get-involved/) so we can put it right.
