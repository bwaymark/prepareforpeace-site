# Growing, keeping and sharing food

A community that can grow some of its own food, keep it safely and share it fairly is far better placed to get through hard times. It is also healthier and more connected in good times. This section covers food from seed to table, with advice suited to the UK climate and UK law.

- Saving, storing and swapping seeds
- Fruit trees, soft fruit and perennial crops that come back every year
- Community gardens and sharing land

How to keep food safe and make a harvest last through the winter.

- Bees and honey
- Rabbits, ducks and goats
- Feeding, housing and animal welfare
