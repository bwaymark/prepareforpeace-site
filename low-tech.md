# No power, no signal: staying in touch the old way

The most reliable ways of keeping in touch need no power and no equipment at all. People managed for centuries with noticeboards, messengers and agreed meeting places, and these still work when everything else has failed. They also include people who do not use phones or the internet.

## Guides we plan to include

- **Paper contact lists.** Keeping a printed list of neighbours, their needs and their skills, and keeping it private and up to date.
- **Phone trees and runners.** Passing a message along a street quickly, with or without phones.
- **Noticeboards.** Where to put one, what to post, and how to keep information accurate.
- **Meeting points.** Agreeing where people gather and at what times when there is no other way to talk.
- **Window signs.** Simple cards that let a household say "we are fine" or "we need help".
- **Local maps.** Marking water points, warm spaces and the homes of people who may need a check.
- **Rumour control.** How to check a message before you pass it on.
