# Domestic abuse: getting help

Domestic abuse often gets worse in hard times, when people are under strain, money is short and families are stuck together at home. It happened during the Covid lockdowns, and it is likely to happen in any long emergency. Being ready includes knowing where to turn, for yourself or for someone you are worried about.

Domestic abuse is not only physical violence. It includes threats, controlling behaviour, cutting someone off from friends and family, and controlling their money. It can happen to anyone, of any age, gender or background.

## Free, confidential helplines

- **England:** National Domestic Abuse Helpline, run by Refuge, 0808 2000 247, open 24 hours.
- **Scotland:** Domestic Abuse and Forced Marriage Helpline, 0800 027 1234, open 24 hours.
- **Wales:** Live Fear Free, 0808 80 10 800, open 24 hours.
- **Northern Ireland:** Domestic and Sexual Abuse Helpline, 0808 802 1414, open 24 hours.
- **Men experiencing abuse:** Men's Advice Line, 0808 801 0327.
- **LGBT+ people:** Galop, 0800 999 5428.
- **If you are worried about your own behaviour:** Respect phoneline, 0808 802 4040.

## If you are worried about someone else

- Stay in touch, and let them know you are there for them, without judging or pushing.
- Do not confront the person you think is abusive, as it can make things more dangerous.
- Help them find out about the helplines above, which also give advice to friends and family.
- If you think someone is in immediate danger, call 999.
